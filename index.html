💌 My Heart's Letter to You

A letter I have been meaning to write for so long, Baby.

💜

My dearest Baby Julie,

I am writing this because there are feelings inside me that words spoken out loud could never fully capture. There is so much I want you to know — not to overwhelm you, not to put any kind of pressure on your heart, but simply because you deserve to know how sincerely and deeply I feel for you.

I miss you, Baby. More than I usually let on, and in ways that are hard to explain. I miss talking to you — those conversations where time somehow disappears, and I realize hours have passed and I still did not want to stop. I miss your voice, the way it sounds when you are telling me something you are excited about, and even the quiet moments when you are just there, being yourself. Even your silences are something I have grown fond of, because just being around you — in any form — already makes everything feel a little warmer and a little brighter.

There are ordinary moments throughout my day when I suddenly think of you for no particular reason at all. I could be doing something completely unrelated to anything, and out of nowhere, my mind drifts to you. Maybe because a song plays and it sounds like something you would like. Maybe because something funny happens and my first instinct is to wish I could tell you about it. Maybe because the sky looks especially beautiful and I immediately think you would have loved seeing it too. You have quietly become such a natural part of how I experience the world around me, Baby, and I think that is one of the most beautiful and unexpected things that has ever happened to me.

Missing you sometimes comes with a kind of ache — a gentle, bittersweet one — where I just wish so much that I could suddenly appear beside you, wrap my arms around you, and give you the longest, warmest hug. No need for big words or grand gestures. Just being close to you would be more than enough. I think about that sometimes, and it honestly makes me smile even as it makes me miss you more.

You have become someone incredibly special to me, Baby Julie. And when I say that, I want you to know I do not use that word lightly. I do not call just anyone special. But you — the way you are, the kindness in your heart, the realness of your personality, the little things you do without even realizing how significant they are — all of it has slowly but surely made you one of the most important people in my life. I am genuinely grateful to God that our paths crossed. I do not think that was an accident, and I carry that gratitude with me every single day.

Baby, I want you to know something that I hope comes through in every little action I take, every message I send, every effort I make to show up for you: I do not want to love you only when things are easy and beautiful. Anybody can be there during the good days. What I want — what I am sincerely asking God to give me the grace to do — is to keep choosing you through the ordinary days, the complicated ones, the misunderstandings, the moments when we might frustrate each other, the seasons when life feels heavy and nothing seems to go right. I want to be the kind of person who stays, who tries, and who keeps showing up — not because everything is perfect, but because you are worth showing up for.

I want to continue courting you, Baby. Sincerely, patiently, and consistently. Not just in words but in the small and steady actions that I hope will show you over time that my heart is genuine. I know that trust is something earned slowly, not demanded, and I respect that completely. I am not in a hurry in a way that disregards your feelings. I just want to keep moving forward, keep being consistent, and keep showing you that what I feel for you is not a passing phase. It is something I am choosing to nurture and protect every single day.

I will never try to rush you, my love. Your heart is your own, and your timeline is your own, and I respect both deeply. Whatever pace you need, I will walk at that pace beside you. I will respect your decisions, your boundaries, your emotions, and your own journey — even the parts that have nothing to do with me. I want you to feel safe, not pressured. I want you to feel appreciated, not obligated. I want anything beautiful that grows between us to grow naturally, freely, and in its own time.

There is a dream I carry in my heart, Baby — and I want to be honest with you about it because I think you deserve that honesty. I dream of the day when you might feel ready to call me your boyfriend. Not because I asked you to, not because you felt like you had to, but because your own heart genuinely wanted to — because you looked at everything we have shared and thought, "Yes. Him. I want to choose him too." That thought alone is enough to make me want to be a better person, a more patient man, and a more intentional version of myself every single day.

And Baby, if God allows it — if our hearts keep growing closer, if our story continues to unfold in the beautiful direction I am hoping for — I dare to dream even further. I dream of someday asking you to marry me. I dream of standing before God with you, making promises that I intend to keep for the rest of my life. I dream of calling you my wife, of building a home with you — not a perfect home, but a real one. A home filled with prayer, laughter, honest conversations, shared meals, and a love that keeps choosing itself even when it is not easy.

I dream of praying with you at the start and end of every day. Of supporting your dreams the way I hope you might support mine. Of being your comfort when the world feels too heavy, and of celebrating with you when everything goes beautifully. I want us to grow together — not just as partners, but as people. I want to become a better man because of the love I have for you. And I want to keep learning how to love you better, every season and every stage of life that we are blessed to share.

I also want you to know, Baby Julie, that you do not need to be perfect to be loved by me. I am not looking for someone without flaws, without difficult days, without moments of doubt or struggle. I am looking for someone real, someone I can be real with — and that is already you. I want to know your imperfections and still choose you. I want to understand your past, your fears, your hopes, and your little quirks, and I want to love all of it. Not in spite of who you are, but because of who you are. I want to keep learning you, all of you, and I do not think I will ever stop finding reasons to be grateful that you are the person I chose to open my heart to.

I am so thankful for every little thing you do. For the time you give me, even when you are busy. For the kindness you show, even in the smallest gestures. For the way you make me feel seen and heard. For the happiness you bring into my life simply by existing in it. I hope you know, my favorite girl, that none of those things go unnoticed. Every little moment matters to me more than you probably realize.

Even right now, even as I write this and I cannot be beside you — my affection for you is here, fully and completely. Distance is just a physical reality; it does not change what I feel. My sincerity is not dependent on how close or how far we are. And even during the times when life gets busy or our conversations are fewer, please know that you are never far from my thoughts, and you are never missing from my prayers.

I pray for you often, Baby. I pray that your days are good, that you are healthy and rested, that the things weighing on you become lighter, and that God is gentle with you in every season of your life. I pray that you feel loved — not just by me, but by the people around you and by God who made you so wonderfully. You deserve to be surrounded by warmth and goodness, always.

So here is my promise to you, Baby Julie. Not a promise of perfection, because I know I will sometimes fall short. But a promise of intention. A promise that I will keep trying. I will keep being sincere with you, keep showing up for you in the ways I can, keep praying for you, and keep choosing you — not just when it is convenient, but especially when it is not. I promise to be patient with your heart, to be gentle with your feelings, and to never take the trust you place in me for granted.

I hope that one day, all of these words, all of these small and consistent actions, and all of the sincerity I carry for you will make sense to your heart in the most beautiful way.

I miss you so, so much, Baby Julie. More than you know. And every single day that I get to know you a little more is another day that makes me quietly, deeply thankful for you.

All my love, sincerity, and patience,
The guy who misses you more than words 💜
